How to play

  • Use the arrow keys, WASD, or swipe to slide every tile in one direction.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into one worth double, and that value is added to your score.
  • Every move drops a new 2 or 4 onto a free square.
  • Make a 2048 tile to win — then keep going for a higher score if you like.
  • It ends when the board is full and no neighbouring tiles match.

Tip: pick one corner and keep your biggest tile parked there.
