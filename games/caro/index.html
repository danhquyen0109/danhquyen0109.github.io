How to play

  • Players take turns placing X and O on the 15×15 board.
  • First to get five in a row — across, down or diagonally — wins.
  • With “Blocked five doesn't win” on (the common Vietnamese rule), a five with the opponent's stones capping both ends does not count.
  • The first move alternates every game.
  • Online: create a room, send the link to a friend and wait for them to open it. The game runs directly between your two browsers — there is no game server.

Tip: an open four (four with both ends empty) can't be stopped. Watch for open threes and block them early.
