How to play

  1. It is your turn when one of your soldiers is ringed on the battlefield. The shot starts from that soldier.
  2. Type a function of x and press Fire. Your shot traces y = f(x), where x is the distance travelled from your soldier — so 0 is a straight line, 0.1*x^2 arcs upward, -2*sin(x) snakes.
  3. Angle rotates the whole curve, and the arrows flip which way you shoot.
  4. The shot dies on terrain or when it leaves the field. Anything caught in the blast dies — including your own soldiers.
  5. Wipe out the other team to win.

Functions you can use

+ - * / ^ % · sin cos tan · asin acos atan atan2 · sinh cosh tanh · exp ln log log2 log10 · sqrt cbrt abs sign · floor ceil round trunc · min max mod pow · pi e

Implicit multiplication works: 2x, 3sin(x) and (x+1)(x-1) all parse — but a variable pushed straight against a name needs a star, so write x*sin(x), not xsin(x). Angles are in radians, and log is the natural log (use log10 for base 10).
